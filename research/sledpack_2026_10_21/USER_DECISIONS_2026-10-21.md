# User decisions on SledPack (2026-10-21, verbatim intent) → SIM-034

1. Stowing: the kite does not need to be stowed. It can stay **unfolded on the back** when not in use. Chairlift use need not
   be considered. If it can fold up and fit in a backpack, even better (bonus, not required).
2. **No pole.** User experience comes first. It is acceptable if the line **briefly** comes within 1 m of the head now and
   then. The kite should **fly higher and farther away when the rider is fast and the wind is strong**.
3. **Tailwind limitation accepted**: if there isn't enough lift, the kite simply flies lower / closer (or not at all).
4. A **fixed filming angle is fine**. If it adds little cost/complexity, automatically pulling the lines to move the kite a
   little left/right/up/down would be nice.
5. The promo video must show these usability and functionality improvements.
